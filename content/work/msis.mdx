## Context

MSIS is a lab management and analytics platform for institutes. As its sole PM, I lead a cross-functional team of 6 across engineering, design, QA, DevOps and subject-matter experts.

## What I did

### Unblocked engineering with a technical spec
The AI direction had left engineering in weeks of ambiguity. I wrote a technical PRD and an MCP-based architecture spec for the **MSIS AI Operating Layer**: a natural-language querying layer plus a phased AI Copilot roadmap.

### Pivoted strategy from what customers actually do
The plan was to expand into a full platform. Five customer interviews and two on-site lab visits showed how institutes really budget for and integrate tools. That led to a pivot to an **API-based modular architecture** that fits their buying reality.

### Replaced static designs with clickable prototypes
Client feedback on static design files took weeks. I prototyped UI revamps with Claude Code as interactive mock websites that clients could click through and react to directly, which cut **feedback cycles from weeks to 2 days**.

## Outcome

- A clear AI architecture and roadmap engineering could build against
- A strategy grounded in how customers buy, not how we hoped they would
- Faster, better client feedback through working prototypes
