## The problem

YPC's internship programme ran on scattered tools and manual judgement. Bonus decisions were inconsistent, and as I dug in, it turned out hiring, project tracking and document management had the same issue: no shared visibility and no historical record.

## What I built

As sole builder and PM, I built YPC Workspace with Claude Code: one platform for hiring, project tracking and internship management, used by 30+ people every day.

## Key decisions

### Security belongs in the database, not the UI
During manual testing I found a critical gap: permissions were enforced in the interface but not underneath it. I moved role-based permissions to the database level, which closed unauthorised access across all 5 user roles.

### Replace gut calls with a transparent formula
Intern bonuses were decided case by case. I designed a quantitative eligibility engine that ranks interns on a weighted mix of task completion rate, engagement and timeliness. Selecting the top-35% cohort became objective and auditable.

### Expand scope when the problem is bigger than the brief
The brief was internship management. Once I saw that hiring, projects and documents all lacked shared visibility, I expanded the product into a full company workspace rather than shipping another single-purpose tool.

## Outcome

- 30+ daily active users across hiring, projects and internships
- Access control enforced at the database level across 5 roles
- Bonus selection that anyone can audit
