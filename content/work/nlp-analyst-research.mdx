## The problem

Research on analyst experience across financial services firms depended on reading Glassdoor reviews and pulling out keywords by hand in Excel. It was slow, inconsistent, and limited how much data the research could use.

## What I built

Working independently, I built a **Python NLP pipeline** using spaCy for language processing and YAKE for keyword extraction. It processed **18,900+ Glassdoor records** and replaced the manual Excel process entirely.

## Outcome

- Research conclusions reached faster, on far more data than manual review allowed
- A repeatable pipeline instead of a one-off spreadsheet exercise
