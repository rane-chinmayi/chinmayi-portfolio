## Context

Elsa is a home-based healthcare robot developed at Oklahoma State University. I was selected for the **NSF I-Corps** programme to test whether it could become a viable commercial product.

## What I did

- Worked in a 4-member team running **100+ customer interviews** across the elder-care ecosystem
- Introduced an **AI simulation layer** to pressure-test and validate what we heard in interviews
- Shaped a **B2B and B2C go-to-market strategy** and a **dual-revenue pricing model**

## What it taught me

Building for elderly users is high-stakes: reliability and trust matter more than features. That lens shapes how I think about AI products today, from confidence scoring to when something is ready to ship.
